# OOi 3D Model — The Wheel

## The object IS a wheel

Every OOi object is a wheel. Not a metaphor — the geometry IS the data structure.

### Front view (L0 — looking at the face)
- **Hub** = OOi center (gold seal, the truth)
- **7 spokes** = 7 00i questions (Giver → Teaching)
- **Rings between spokes** = cells (filled with data, not empty)
- **Outer rim** = L0 (the ledger, holds everything together)
- **Metal brackets** where spokes meet rim = interconnections to other objects

### Side view (hourglass — looking at the edge)
- **Narrow top** = L1 (7 cells, identity)
- **Expanding** = L2 (49), L3 (343)
- **Widest band** = L4 (2,401 cells, equator — the structural metal band)
- **Narrowing** = L5 (343), L6 (49)
- **Narrow bottom** = L7 (7 cells, gold seal)
- **Wood planks** between metal bands = data compartments per layer
- **Spoke crossbars** visible on the side = 7 factors per layer
- **Gaps you can see through** = Z axis (depth = time, darker = older)

### Z depth (looking through the gaps)
- **Front** = NOW (bright, recent data, high truth.rate)
- **Middle** = PAST (dimmer, older data, decaying truth.rate)
- **Deep** = OLD (faint, stale, approaching archive)
- **Gone** = Z (archived, invisible, moved to _archive/)

## 8 Wheels — The Octants

One object has 8 wheels stacked on the same axle.

The Three 7s create 3 axes:
- **WHO** (L1-L7) — values and trust
- **WHAT** (7 pillars) — Brand · Products · Sales · Community · People · Capital · Operations
- **WHERE** (P1-P7) — Formula · Product · Market · Brand · Ops · Tech · Archive

Each axis divides at the midpoint (L4, pillar 4, P4):
- 2 × 2 × 2 = **8 octants = 8 wheels**

```

  ╔══════╗  Octant 1: WHO high + WHAT high + WHERE high (deep trust, strategic, tech)
  ║ data ║
  ╠══════╣  ← metal band (interconnection)
  ║ data ║  Octant 2: WHO high + WHAT high + WHERE low (deep trust, strategic, formula)
  ╠══════╣
  ║ data ║  Octant 3: WHO high + WHAT low + WHERE high (deep trust, operational, tech)
  ╠══════╣
  ║ data ║  Octant 4: WHO high + WHAT low + WHERE low (deep trust, operational, formula)
  ╠══════╣  ← EQUATOR (the big metal band — WHO midpoint)
  ║ data ║  Octant 5: WHO low + WHAT high + WHERE high (surface, strategic, tech)
  ╠══════╣
  ║ data ║  Octant 6: WHO low + WHAT high + WHERE low (surface, strategic, formula)
  ╠══════╣
  ║ data ║  Octant 7: WHO low + WHAT low + WHERE high (surface, operational, tech)
  ╠══════╣
  ║ data ║  Octant 8: WHO low + WHAT low + WHERE low (surface, operational, formula)
  ╔══════╗

  ◀──── axle = OOi identity (runs through all 8) ────▶
```
