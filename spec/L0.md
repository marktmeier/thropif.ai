## L0 is not a data layer. L0 is the RECORD of all data layers.

- L1-L7 hold the data.
- L0 records every change to that data.
- L0 is append-only. Immutable. The blockchain.
- When truth.rate changes in any cell at any layer, L0 logs it.
- L0 IS the truth — not because it computes truth, but because it records every claim and every verification.

## >MOSTixOOi7x7

L0 contains the MOST — the master index of the OOi 7×7:
- Every object registered
- Every cell address ever written
- Every truth.rate transition (empty → draft → review → verified)
- Every connection in the mesh
- Every timestamp

## The top-down view

Looking down at the disc from L0:
- L1 is the outer ring (largest circumference, fewest cells)
- L7 is the center (smallest, the gold seal)
- The hourglass inverts: what was the widest layer (L4) in the side view becomes the middle ring in the top view
- ALL 3,199 cells are visible simultaneously
- The gradient (00i cyan → OOi gold) flows from the edge (L1) to the center (L7)

This is why "geometry is address" — from L0, the position of every dot on the disc IS its canonical address. No lookup needed. The disc IS the database.
